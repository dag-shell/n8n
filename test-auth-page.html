						Why is n8n-auth not in Document Cookies?

						POST /rest/login sets n8n-auth with the HttpOnly flag.
						By browser security design, JavaScript (document.cookie) is blocked from reading HttpOnly cookies.
						The cookie IS stored in your browser — you can see it in DevTools: F12 > Application > Cookies > http://localhost:5678.
